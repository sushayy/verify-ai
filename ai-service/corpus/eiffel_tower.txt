The Eiffel Tower is a wrought-iron lattice tower located on the Champ de Mars
in Paris, France. It was designed by engineer Gustave Eiffel and completed in
1889 as the entrance arch for the World's Fair. Standing at approximately 330
meters tall, it was the tallest man-made structure in the world for 41 years.
Today it remains one of the most visited paid monuments in the world and is
one of the most recognizable landmarks of Paris and of France as a whole.
