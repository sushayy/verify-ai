Mount Everest, located in the Mahalangur Himal sub-range of the Himalayas, is
Earth's highest mountain above sea level, with a peak at approximately 8,849
meters. It sits on the border between Nepal and the Tibet Autonomous Region
of China. The mountain attracts hundreds of climbers each year during a
narrow spring climbing window, and ascents carry significant risk due to
altitude, weather, and terrain.
