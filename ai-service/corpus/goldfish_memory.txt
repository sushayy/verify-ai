The popular belief that goldfish have a three-second memory is false.
Laboratory studies have repeatedly demonstrated that goldfish can retain
learned associations for weeks and in some cases months. Researchers have
trained goldfish to press levers for food at specific times of day, to
navigate mazes, and to distinguish between shapes and colours, with the
learned behaviour persisting long after training stopped. One frequently
cited study showed goldfish recalling a trained escape route after a gap of
several months. The three-second figure does not appear in any peer-reviewed
literature and appears to be folklore.
