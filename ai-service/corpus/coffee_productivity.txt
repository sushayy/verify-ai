Numerous studies have examined the relationship between caffeine consumption
and workplace performance. Caffeine is a mild central nervous system
stimulant and has been shown in some studies to modestly improve alertness,
reaction time, and short-term focus for many people. However, the effect
size varies widely between individuals, tasks, and study designs, and
researchers have not established a single universal productivity gain figure
that applies across all workplaces or all employees. Some studies show small
improvements, others show no significant effect, and heavy or late-day
consumption has been associated with sleep disruption that can reduce
next-day performance.
