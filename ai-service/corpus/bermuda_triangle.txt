The Bermuda Triangle, an area of the western North Atlantic between Florida,
Bermuda, and Puerto Rico, has no statistically elevated rate of ship or
aircraft disappearances. Insurance underwriters including Lloyd's of London
do not charge higher premiums for transit through the region, and the US
Coast Guard does not recognize it as a distinct hazard area. Reviews of the
commonly cited cases have found that many were inaccurately reported: some
vessels disappeared far outside the region, some occurred in documented
severe weather, and a few were never lost at all. The area is heavily
trafficked and subject to the Gulf Stream and sudden tropical storms, which
accounts for the incidents that did occur without invoking anything unusual.
