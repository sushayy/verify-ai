A persistent popular myth holds that the Great Wall of China is visible to the
naked eye from the Moon. This claim is false. The Great Wall is only about
5 to 9 meters wide in most sections, far too narrow to be resolved by the
human eye from a distance of roughly 384,000 kilometers. Multiple astronauts,
including those from the Apollo missions, have confirmed that no man-made
structures on Earth are visible to the naked eye from the Moon. The wall is
difficult to see even from low Earth orbit without magnification, and it is
not distinguishable from surrounding terrain in most unaided orbital photos.
